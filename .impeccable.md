## Design Context

### Users
People who want a **spectacle toy**, not a policy workstation. They land cold from a link or trend chip, pick a fight, crown a winner, share the knockout.

### Brand Personality
**Theatrical · Competitive · Sharp**

### Aesthetic Direction
**Chamber poster on concrete** — print energy for LinkedIn, thumb-first on mobile.

- Cool concrete field, ink type, hazard-yellow CTA, cobalt/crimson party stamps
- Softened game language: chamber / floor / verdict (not bloodsport)
- Mobile: no clipping brand, 48px+ tap targets, sticky console, horizontal fighter rail
- LinkedIn Featured: absolute `og:image` at 1200×630 (`/og-card.png` on congressai.app)

### Design Principles
1. Brand is the hero on first viewport
2. One job per screen
3. Shareable climax every bout
4. Speed is part of fun
5. Never apologize for the engine — the floor just speaks
6. Harden for chaos (long topics, mobile, reduced motion)
