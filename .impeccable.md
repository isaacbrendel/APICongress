## Design Context

### Users
People who want a **spectacle toy**, not a policy workstation. They land cold from a link or trend chip, pick a fight, crown a winner, share the knockout.

### Brand Personality
**Theatrical · Competitive · Sharp**

### Aesthetic Direction
**Fight Card on Concrete** — print-shop poster energy, not dark SaaS.

- Atmosphere: cool concrete field, ink-black type, hazard-yellow CTA, cobalt/crimson stamps for parties
- Typography: condensed display (Syne) + tight sans (Geist-alternative: Outfit stays but heavier contrast) + ink serif for quotes
- No glassmorphism, no brass-on-navy “AI luxury,” no purple glow, no nested cards
- Motion: stamp-in cast, score ticks, share poster reveal
- Continuity: chamber always speaks; never advertise demo/fallback modes

### Design Principles
1. Brand is the hero on first viewport
2. One job per screen
3. Shareable climax every bout
4. Speed is part of fun
5. Never apologize for the engine — the floor just speaks
6. Harden for chaos (long topics, mobile, reduced motion)
